Chef Zay

Private Chef • Steak Specialist • New Mexico Roots

Crafted in New Mexico

Born in Taos and based in Albuquerque, Chef Zay brings the spirit of New Mexico to every plate. With years of culinary dedication and formal training, he specializes in elevated, steak-forward cuisine using 100% grass-fed beef from local farms like Sweet Mercy Farms, No Bull Prime, MAS Cattle, and Nelson Meats.

Signature techniques — sous vide precision, high-heat flame grilling, and custom compound butters (including the fan-favorite Lemon Pepper Rainbow Trout) — deliver restaurant-quality results in your home.

Culinary Philosophy

Every menu follows animal-based principles — nose-to-tail eating with premium proteins, organs, raw dairy, fruit, and honey. All sourcing prioritizes local Albuquerque farms and markets for the freshest, highest-quality ingredients.
